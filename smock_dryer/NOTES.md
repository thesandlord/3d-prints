# Smock dryer

A counter stand. The hanger is a bar with a hole in the middle. It slides onto a post on the wide upper tube. The slim lower tube has a square tenon that seats in the middle of an X made from two feet. The hanger sits 440 mm off the counter.

The two tubes nest. The slim one slides up inside the wide one. A flare at the top of the slim tube is wider than the mouth, and four slits in the mouth let it spring through. Pulled down, the flare seats in the mouth and holds the stand open. The hanger and both feet go inside the slim tube.

## Parts
Print `foot.stl` twice.

| File | Qty | Prints as |
|---|---|---|
| `stl/spine_lower.stl` | 1 | Standing on the tenon, 62 × 62 × 214 mm with the brim |
| `stl/spine_upper.stl` | 1 | Standing on the mouth, 68 × 68 × 244 mm with the brim |
| `stl/hanger.stl` | 1 | Flat, 200 × 22 × 5 mm |
| `stl/foot.stl` | 2 | Flat, 200 × 20 × 8 mm |

The brim and the ribs on the slim tube snap off. They are only there so the tenon, which is 11 mm across, does not get knocked off the bed while the tube is still short. Snap them off flush before the tenon goes into the feet. The wide tube's brim snaps off the same way. Its mouth is already a 48 mm ring, so it only needs the flat brim.

The slim cavity is 25.6 × 25.6 × 204 mm, which holds the 200 mm hanger and both 200 mm feet.

0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, about 30% infill. No slicer supports. Print both tubes standing up. PETG if it lives next to the sink. PLA is fine on a dry counter.

## Use
1. Cross the two feet. One slot faces up, the other faces down.
2. Pull the slim tube down out of the wide one until the flare seats in the mouth.
3. Set the tenon into the hole at the middle of the X.
4. Slide the hanger onto the post. It stops on the flat at the top of the wide tube.
5. To store it, take the hanger and the feet off, slide them into the slim tube from the top, then push the slim tube up into the wide one until the flare pops through the mouth.
