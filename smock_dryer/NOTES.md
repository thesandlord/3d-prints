# Smock dryer

A counter stand. The hanger is a bar with a hole in the middle. It slides down a post on top of the spine and stops on the collar. The spine's tenon drops into the middle of an X made from two feet. The shoulders sit 392 mm off the counter.

The spine prints as two tubes. The upper one has a plug with two hooks that snap into windows in the lower tube. Take the upper tube off and the hanger and both feet slide into the lower cavity.

## Parts
Print `foot.stl` twice.

| File | Qty | Prints as |
|---|---|---|
| `stl/spine_lower.stl` | 1 | Standing on the tenon, 32 × 32 × 246 mm |
| `stl/spine_upper.stl` | 1 | Standing on the plug, 36 × 36 × 172 mm |
| `stl/hanger.stl` | 1 | Flat, 200 × 22 × 5 mm |
| `stl/foot.stl` | 2 | Flat, 200 × 20 × 8 mm |

The lower tube is the longest piece. Its cavity is 25.6 × 25.6 × 220 mm, which holds the 200 mm hanger and both 200 mm feet side by side.

0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, about 30% infill. No supports. Print both spine tubes standing up. PETG if it lives next to the sink. PLA is fine on a dry counter.

## Use
1. Cross the two feet. One slot faces up, the other faces down. They drop together into a flush X.
2. Push the upper tube's plug into the lower tube until the two hooks click.
3. Set the tenon into the hole at the middle of the X.
4. Slide the hanger onto the post. It stops on the collar. The post sticks out the top.
5. To store it, lift off the hanger, pull the spine out of the feet, unsnap the upper tube, and slide the hanger and both feet into the lower tube.
